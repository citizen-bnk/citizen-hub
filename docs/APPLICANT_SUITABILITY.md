# Recruitment suitability and guest access

Citizen Digital Ltd’s application journey uses neutral professional wording. It does not announce an unconfirmed future business in the questionnaire, misrepresent the employer, promise a licence, or hide authorised regulatory use of information. Public source advertisements remain available and unchanged.

Board and senior management positions collect preliminary declarations concerning integrity, professional discipline/disqualification, relevant misconduct/legal proceedings, financial soundness, governance and conflicting interests. Applicants can disclose context or request a confidential discussion. A “yes” never automatically rejects a candidate. Human evaluators review declarations and supporting evidence.

These questions map to the themes in CBL’s published bank-establishment requirements and Key Management Officials questionnaire. This is preliminary recruitment screening, not the statutory questionnaire, a complete fit-and-proper assessment or CBL approval. The appropriate formal submission and additional certified evidence must be confirmed with CBL for each relevant role. Sensitive medical, asset/liability, identity-number and tax details are not solicited in the initial text questions.

The selfie and photo holding ID are Citizen recruitment identity-evidence requirements; they are not described as mandated by CBL. They remain in private Drive storage and require human review. The AI helper receives only the selected vacancy, current question and the user’s explicit help message; application answers and photos are not automatically included. There are no ranking or selection tools.

Guest applicants can submit without accounts. Optional registration uses the existing Stack Auth password/verification flow after saving the application; passwords never enter institutional application rows. A failed registration does not discard a saved application. A supplied email is unverified until authenticated provider verification; only explicitly verified provider emails can link pending applications to an account. Matching email alone grants no session, administrative access or roles. Record snapshots stay marked unverified until this step.

Administrators can review unregistered applications and progress early screening. New offers require contact verification and all required evidence. Employment and board approval gates continue to apply; the system grants no appointment roles automatically.

Operational dependencies: private Drive OAuth connection and reviewer folder permissions; configured shared Core AI provider for generative chat; an approved recruitment retention/privacy process. No promise of successful uploads or AI generation is made while those dependencies are absent.
